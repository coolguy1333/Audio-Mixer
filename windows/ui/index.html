Find sounds online?

Search MyInstants, Freesound, Openverse and Wikimedia Commons, or paste a link, and add sounds with one click. Your searches are sent to the site you pick; nothing is sent until you turn this on. You can turn it off in Settings.

Settings

Use it in Discord, OBS or a game

1. Install the free VB-Cable. 2. Set Plays on to CABLE Input. 3. In Discord (or the game), set your input device to CABLE Output. 4. Set Mic passthrough to your microphone, so they hear you and the sounds. 5. Set Also hear it on to your headphones to hear the sounds yourself.
